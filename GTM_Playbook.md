# GTM Playbook — Ambition for Korea
Fill in on the day, once the mission and MVP are defined.

## 0. Inputs
Mission · Problem · MVP. Every step below derives from these three.

## 1. ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
Derived from the problem and the product: who feels the pain most and can pay.
- Segment, persona and context (when the problem appears)
- Current workaround and why it fails
- Willingness to pay and where they gather
- B2B only: industry, company size, decision maker vs. user

## 2. USPs (2–3)
Built from the ICP's pain vs. alternatives.
Template: *For [ICP] who [pain], we [benefit], unlike [alternative], because [proof].*

## 3. Channels
Where the ICP already is. Pick 1–2 we can actually test.
- B2C: app stores, creators, communities, referrals
- B2B: LinkedIn outbound, partners/resellers, marketplaces, events

## 4. AARRR
One metric + one tactic per stage.
- **Acquisition**: where users come from
- **Activation**: the "aha" moment
- **Retention**: why they come back
- **Revenue**: pricing model
- **Referral**: why they invite others

## 5. Validation
- **B2B → PoV (Proof of Value)**: success metric agreed with the customer, 2–4 week pilot, baseline vs. result, ROI, path to paid contract
- **B2C → Continuous improvement**: cohort retention, A/B tests on onboarding and paywall, review mining, weekly build–measure–learn loop

## 6. Market sizing
TAM / SAM / SOM. Revenue = reachable customers × conversion × price.
Label every number as **fact**, **evidence** or **hypothesis**.

## 7. Investment roadmap
- **Angel / Pre-seed**: team, problem, prototype
- **Seed**: early traction and PMF signals (retention, first revenue)
- **Series A**: repeatable growth and unit economics (CAC vs. LTV)

## Research sources (starting point)
- Market size: Statista, Straits Research, Grand View Research
- App revenue/downloads: Sensor Tower, Appfigures, Business of Apps
- Startups/funding: Crunchbase, Dealroom, CB Insights
- Demand signals: Google Trends, Similarweb, App Store/Google Play reviews, Reddit
- Macro/consumer data: OECD, World Bank, Pew Research
- Research: Google Scholar, PubMed

*Not exhaustive. The right source depends on the question, so rethink it before each research task (e.g., Spain's public tender database for a B2G question).*
